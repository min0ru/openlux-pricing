# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized language or imagery, trolling, public or private intimidation, and
other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to 2010min0ru@gmail.com. All reports are reviewed
and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participation for behavior
that violates this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
